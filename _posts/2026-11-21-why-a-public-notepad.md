*Starter note. Rewrite this in your own words or delete the file.*

This is where I put things before they're finished: half-formed ideas, notes from client work (scrubbed), things I got wrong, and things I'm building.

Writing in public forces a little more clarity than a private doc does, and it's a better record than my memory.

To add a note, drop a markdown file in `_posts/` named `YYYY-MM-DD-some-title.md` with a `title` at the top. GitHub rebuilds the site on push.
